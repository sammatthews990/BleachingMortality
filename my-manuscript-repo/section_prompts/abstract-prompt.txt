Use `applied-ecology-writing` to draft a structured Abstract and Significance Statement directly into `manuscript.qmd`.

Inputs & Workspace Inspection:
1. Review the existing Introduction, Methods, and Results sections in `manuscript.qmd`.
2. Inspect the core summary metrics and figure outputs in `./output/`.

Drafting Specifications:
- Abstract Structure (150–200 words, Nature/Conservation Letters style):
  * Sentence 1–2: Broad ecological imperative and foundational context.
  * Sentence 3: The critical knowledge gap or policy/management bottleneck.
  * Sentence 4–5: Methodological approach and quantitative study scope.
  * Sentence 6–7: Primary empirical findings with exact effect sizes/metrics.
  * Sentence 8: Concrete conservation and policy implications.
- Significance Statement (100–120 words):
  * Explain in plain, punchy language why these findings matter to broad ecology and real-world conservation decision-makers.

Style & Constraints:
- Eliminate nominalizations and passive constructions.
- Match all reported numbers exactly with workspace data outputs.
