2. **Strengthen the selected operational COTS pressure at the severe tail.** The RRN event-season density is now treated as pre-bleaching pressure because it has no specific within-season survey date; exact-date Manta and culling records remain limited to the end of February. Raw RRN excess plus latest/prior-three-year Manta state improves Gannett and severe RMSE while retaining aggregate transfer, but still predicts only about 0.183 of Gannett's 0.566 loss. Retain the log RRN form as a severe-tail sensitivity, develop a dated in-event COTS update or outbreak-propagation nowcast, and keep targeted culling removals as context unless effort and targeting can be modelled explicitly.
3. **Publish early observations as a separate condition layer, not a mortality correction.** The reliability-weighted, event-centred aerial/RHIS anomaly points in the expected residual direction in most events, but sector-excluded RMSE improves only 0.00030, block-excluded RMSE worsens 0.00006, and the locked 2025 both-design guard fails. Keep the update off by default. Preserve its single non-negative occurrence effect, no-intercept form for future-event validation rather than expanding the predictor set.
4. **Do not promote the current ENSO occurrence correction; improve event-state data first.** RONI/SOI with ERA5 rainfall and WQC helps 2022 but worsens 2020 and suppresses 2024. Pilot the Queensland WMIP discharge contract in `docs/freshwater-input-contract.md`, audit river-to-reef connectivity, and expand the historical event set before another strongly regularised occurrence-only test. Event identity remains explanatory-only.

8. **Keep the independent direct BRT as a within-event learner, not the initial forecast.** The full-predictor direct and two-part BRTs have now been fit independently of INLA with nested tuning. The direct BRT improves reef-blocked RMSE from 0.1396 to 0.1354, and a predictor-only applicability blend reaches 0.1282, but direct-BRT leave-one-event-out RMSE is 0.1799 versus 0.1485 for INLA and severe RMSE is 0.4650 versus 0.3267. Even inside the event-held-out environmental envelope, INLA remains better (RMSE 0.1354 versus 0.1692). Do not use BRT for an initial forecast or infer that envelope membership guarantees BRT superiority. Retain it for interpolation after an event state is represented, for nonlinear effect diagnostics, and as a candidate in a spatially independent early-event update. Revalidate any future gating rule prospectively and require event-held-out overall and severe-tail gains.
